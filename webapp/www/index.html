Dot

Version 1.0

Dot is an online metronome that aims to be simple, useful and free forever. Set your tempo with the slider, the -5/+5 buttons or tap tempo, and split each beat into subdivisions from 2 to 7. No ads, no tracking, no account, no requests to upgrade to premium.
